> Over half of US counties have **zero OB-GYNs.** Preeclampsia, hemorrhage, and sepsis are detectable hours before onset — but monitoring tools don't reach the communities that need them most.

## The Solution

AI risk-scoring, prenatal monitoring, postpartum warning sign detection, facility routing.

## Agents

**RiskScorer** — Score pregnancy risk from blood pressure trends, lab values, demographics
**PreeclampsiAlert** — Flag preeclampsia 24-72 hours before onset
**PrenatalChecklist** — Personalized care checklist in patient's language
**FacilityRouter** — Route high-risk patients to nearest appropriate facility
**PostpartumMonitor** — SMS check-in agents for postpartum warning signs
**LanguageBridge** — Multilingual patient communication

## Safety

MANDATORY: 911 for hemorrhage/seizure symptoms. 988 for postpartum mental health crisis. This supplements, never replaces, prenatal care.

## Data Sources

CDC WONDER maternal data, ACOG guidelines, WHO ICD-10 codes, state perinatal collaboratives, MIMIC-IV
